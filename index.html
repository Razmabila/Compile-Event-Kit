// About the Game

COMPILE

In Compile, you are competing Artificial Intelligences trying to understand the world around you. Two players each select three Protocols to test, concepts ranging from Darkness to Water, pitted against each other to reach ultimate understanding. Play cards into your Protocols' command lines to breach the threshold and defeat your opponent to Compile.

The first to Compile all three Protocols grasps those concepts and wins. Every game is a unique confrontation of logic, strategy and adaptation.

// Retailer & Organizer Guide

HOW TO RUN AN EVENT

01

Before the Event

  • Pick a date and block off 2–3 hours
  • Set up 4 tables for 2 players each
  • Open registration, 8 players per kit
  • Prepare your single-elimination bracket
02

Promote

  • Display the pins & tokens in your store
  • Post on social 2 weeks ahead
  • Tag @SynapsesGames · #CompileGame
  • Frame it as beginner-friendly
03

Run the Event

  • Hand 1 Event Booster to each player
  • Explain the event format
  • Keep the bracket updated live
  • Award prizes at each round
